Quick Start Guide

Everything you need to sign in and run your week on the LVL/PSK EOS Platform.

1Signing in for the first time

1.Go to https://eos.rnd-loki.com/l10/login (the button in your invite email takes you there).
2.Enter your work email address and the one-time password from the invite email.
3.You will be asked to set your own password before continuing — choose something only you know (6+ characters).
4.That's it — you land on the Dashboard. Next time, sign in with your own password.
Forgot your password? Use Forgot password on the sign-in page and a reset link will be emailed to you. It expires in 1 hour.

2Finding your way around

The navigation bar at the top has a tab for each part of your Level 10 rhythm:

Dashboard — your weekly health at a glance: scorecard status, rocks progress, open to-dos and issues, all in one view.
Scorecard — the measurables you own, week by week. Enter your number for the week and it is instantly scored against goal (on-track shows green, off-track red). Some rows (e.g. revenue) update automatically from company systems.
Rocks — your quarterly priorities. Keep the status honest: On Track or Off Track, and mark milestones done as you complete them.
To-Dos — 7-day commitments from the weekly meeting. Tick them off as you finish; anything older than 7 days stands out.
Issues — the IDS list (Identify, Discuss, Solve). Spot an obstacle during the week? Add it here so it gets solved in the meeting instead of forgotten.
Meeting — coming soon. Guided Level 10 meeting mode is on its way; the tab is visible but not yet active.

3Your weekly rhythm

  • Before the meeting: enter your scorecard numbers, update your rock statuses, and tick off completed to-dos.
  • During the week: log issues as they come up — big or small.
  • Use the + Create button (top right) to quickly add a to-do, issue, rock or headline from anywhere.

4Good to know

  • The platform works on your phone and tablet as well as your computer.
  • You only see data for the companies and departments you have access to.
  • Spotted a bug or have a suggestion? Use the in-app rating prompt, or tell Dennis or Taha.
